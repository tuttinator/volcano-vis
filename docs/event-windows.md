# Window selection

Each volcano owns its window in the generated catalog. Selecting a volcano uses its own editions; a shared September date range is not imposed. Archive availability and eruption onset are different concepts.

- **Anak Krakatau:** selected start 5 September 2026, 00:00 WIB. The replay retains 4 September 22:00 WIB onward as context. The first recovered advisory is 5 September 13:30 WIB (#2026/174), so earlier imagery is not accompanied by an invented advisory. The catalog continues through 12 September.
- **Semeru:** 19–24 November 2025 is now the default explorer window, backed by 30 retained Darwin editions from the [GDACS archive](https://data.gdacs.org/gts.aspx?eventid=1000125&eventtype=VO). The first retained issue is 19 November 09:46 UTC / 16:46 WIB. It reports FL540 and explicitly unavailable forecasts. Later advisories include multiple altitude components and a “NO VA EXP” target, preserved separately from unavailable data. September 2026 remains selectable as a separate event window. Six daily VIIRS crops for 19–24 November accompany the advisories. Exact overpass times are unavailable; these daily mosaics are context, not yet a ten-minute historical satellite replay. [ESDM episode source](https://www.esdm.go.id/id/media-center/arsip-berita/pvmbg-tingkatkan-aktivitas-gunung-api-semeru-ke-level-iv-awas).
- **Merapi:** 12–17 March 2023 is the retained advisory window (10 editions, beginning 12 March 22:50 WIB). [ESDM reports](https://www.esdm.go.id/en/media-center/news-archives/saat-status-siaga-gunung-merapi-alami-rentetan-awan-panas-guguran) an activity increase at 12:12 WIB on 11 March; the archive does not cover that initial onset. Seven daily VIIRS crops cover 11–17 March, including context before the first retained advisory; explorer comparisons offer swipe and side-by-side views. Six initial assessments explicitly say ash was not identifiable in satellite data. These are preserved as uncertainty, not evidence of no ash. Source HTML is retained in `data/raw/gdacs-merapi-2023-gts.html` and `data/raw/reports/esdm-merapi-2023-03-11.html`.
- **Dukono:** 8 May 2026 is a candidate discrete eruption for a short replay. ESDM describes that morning's eruption and an increase beginning 30 March; 30 March would suit a longer activity-history view. Neither should be described as the onset of all Dukono activity. [ESDM source](https://www.esdm.go.id/en/media-center/news-archives/gunung-dukono-kembali-erupsi-masyarakat-diminta-waspadai-sebaran-abu-vulkanik).
- **Lewotolok and Ibu:** retain the available September advisory sequences while an episode with recoverable plume data is selected. A status change or lava-flow increase alone does not guarantee an informative airborne-ash replay.

Only Anak Krakatau currently has a satellite event replay. Semeru has a historical advisory episode; the other volcano windows are labelled as archive sequences, with their actual independent first/last issue times. Candidate historical dates above are editorial research choices, not claims that those datasets have been downloaded.
