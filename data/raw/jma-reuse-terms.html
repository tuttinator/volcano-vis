Japan Meteorological Agency Website Privacy Policy

31 January 2020: partial revision of 2) Scope of Information collected, 3) Purposes of use, and 5) Security measures

 1) Basic policy

The Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) collects information on users of its website within the scope required for optimal provision of services (including the provision of information as well as the collection of feedback and comments via the website). All information collected is used appropriately within the scope of the purposes specified below.

 2) Scope of information collected

  1. This website automatically collects information on Internet domain names, IP addresses, access status and other data (hereinafter referred to as “user information”). Information collected using cookies (sent by the server to identify users via the browser and saved on the user’s computer) is used solely to improve usability, and does not include any personal data. Browsers can be set to disable cookie usage, which does not affect access to the website.
  2. User location information is referenced for tailored map display on some pages of this website. However, JMA does not collect location information. Browsers can be set to disable location information usage, which does not affect access to the website.
  3. Users may be required to register their name, address, age, occupation, phone number, email address and other information (hereinafter referred to as “User Information”) to use certain pages of the website, such as those with feedback and comment functions. The email address of the submitter is displayed on the receiver’s end.
  4. Users may be required to provide User Information in relation to the nature of particular projects implemented by JMA. These may include calls for requests to attend PR and other events, as well as written surveys.

 3) Purposes of use

  1. Information collected as detailed in Item 2) a. is used to improve website accessibility via measures such as recording of appropriate ranges for map coverage, and to provide optimal services.
  2. This website uses Google Analytics, which collects user information using cookies. See the terms of service.
  3. The content of feedback and comments collected as detailed in Item 2) c., including User Information, is used to develop future JMA policies and measures. To this end, such content may be forwarded to relevant JMA divisions. Email addresses, phone numbers and fax numbers may be used to reply to user inquiries and for confirmation-related contact. Information collected may also be forwarded to relevant ministries and agencies depending on the nature of feedback provided or comments made.
  4. Information collected as detailed in Item 2) d. is used only as required for optimal implementation of PR and other events.

 4) Restrictions on the use and provision of information

JMA does not use collected information for purposes other than those specified in Paragraph 3) or provide such to third parties unless information disclosure is required in association with laws or ordinances, violations such as unauthorized access or threats, or other special circumstances. However, statistically processed information on access to this website, on user information and so forth may be published.

 5) Security measures

  1. JMA takes all measures necessary to prevent the leakage, loss or destruction of information collected and to manage such appropriately. Analysis of the status of access to this website and user feedback received may be consigned to external organizations as deemed necessary for commercial operations. In such cases, JMA ensures that the relevant organizations take all measures necessary for appropriate management of the information.
  2. Management of user information collected via Google Analytics is based on the privacy policy of Google Inc. JMA is not liable for any damages resulting from the use of the Google Analytics service.

 6) Collection of information

Except for User Information provided in relation to feedback/comments and requests to attend PR and other events, this website does not collect general information (such as names and dates of birth) that would allow JMA to identify the user.

 7) Scope of application

The privacy policy outlined here applies exclusively to this website. The handling of information on websites of other ministries and agencies is the responsibility of the organization concerned.

 8) Other

JMA may revise its privacy policy from time to time. Any future revisions will be announced on this website.
